MEDICORE HOSPITAL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: DISCHARGE & TRANSFER SOP
Document ID: SOP-DIS-2026-02
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Approved By: Chief Medical Officer

1. DISCHARGE AUTHORIZATION
- Only the attending consultant or designated primary physician may authorize patient discharge by signing the formal Clinical Discharge Order.

2. DISCHARGE SUMMARY REQUIREMENTS
Every discharged patient must receive an authorized, printed Discharge Summary containing:
- Admission and discharge timestamps.
- Primary and secondary diagnoses with ICD-10 codification.
- Summary of hospital course, surgical procedures performed, and significant diagnostic test results.
- Discharge Medication List: exact generic drug names, dosages, frequencies, and durations.
- Clear dietary, lifestyle, and wound-care instructions.
- Date, time, and department for mandatory outpatient follow-up.

3. FINANCIAL & PHARMACY CLEARANCE
- The Nursing Station notifies the Billing & Inpatient Department upon consultant authorization.
- The Hospital Pharmacy conducts medication reconciliation, accepts unused unopened medications for credit, and dispenses take-home prescriptions.
- The Finance Department reconciles interim payments, applies insurance approvals, and issues the Final Receipt and Gate Pass upon full settlement.
