MEDICORE HOSPITAL PHARMACY STANDARD: DISPENSING AND INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
Document ID: SOP-PHARM-2026-04
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Approved By: Chief Pharmacist

1. PRESCRIPTION VALIDATION
- A licensed pharmacist must verify: patient identity, prescribing doctor's registration number, drug name, dosage form, strength, frequency, route, duration, and potential drug-drug interactions or allergies.

2. ATOMIC INVENTORY DISPENSING
- Medicines must be dispensed from batches adhering to First-Expiry-First-Out (FEFO).
- The hospital ERP must execute an atomic inventory decrement simultaneously with the dispensing event.
- If batch stock is less than requested quantity, the dispensing transaction must be halted with an Insufficient Stock warning.

3. NEAR-EXPIRY AND REORDER ALERTS
- Medicines expiring within 90 days must be highlighted in amber in the inventory dashboard.
- Medicines at or below the minimum reorder threshold (default: 20 units) generate automated replenishment requisitions.
